# The global listening party: topology and cost plans

The owner, 23 November 2026: "lets prepare for large listener runs of opus audio - simulating a global listening party with thousands or tens of thousands of users". One stream, 1 ms Opus Custom (`opus-1`) or standard 2.5 ms Opus (`opus-2.5`) over UDP, listeners in London, New York, Tokyo and Sydney, ramped 1,000, 5,000, 10,000, 20,000 and on, joining in waves, an edge and a distributor killed mid-party, the autoscaler adding edges as it grows, self-hosted LiveKit and LiveKit Cloud as controls.

Generated by `scripts/scale_topology.py --write` (with `deploy/low-latency/topology-party.json`, the topology of the 20,000-listener `opus-1` plan with 2 paths); do not edit by hand. Any other plan: `scripts/scale_topology.py plan --listeners N --lane L --paths P --hours H` (every figure below is a flag of it), and its topology: `scripts/scale_topology.py party` with the same flags.

## Assumptions

Planning estimates, not measurements on Linode. The proving run (C-operations 7) measures them; give the measured figures to `plan` and print again.

- **An edge** (g6-dedicated-2) is full at about 400,000 client datagrams a second (wave 7: 3.0-3.3 us a datagram; scale-run's DATAGRAM_CAPACITY). The plan fills it to 70%, the autoscaler's trigger to add an edge (`--edge-datagrams-per-s`, `--edge-fill`). At that fill p99 may not stay within 1 ms of idle: wave 7 put that at 200-250 one-path 1 ms listeners an edge.
- **A leg** (one subscription; a two-path listener is two legs on two edges) is 1.031 datagrams a part (repair rows; C 7: about 1,031 a second at 1 ms) and, on the wire, 1.2 Mbit/s at `opus-1` and 0.6 Mbit/s at `opus-2.5` (scale-run's planning figures; `--datagrams-per-part`, `--leg-mbps`).
- **A listener host** (needletail-load) spends 0.0055 vCPU per one-path listener of 1,000 parts a second (in-process bench on the dev box: 3.96 us a datagram plus about 1.5 us of NIC softirq), scaled by the lane's parts a second and the paths, filled to 70% (`--load-vcpu-per-listener`, `--load-fill`). g6-dedicated-8 hosts, g6-dedicated-4 in sydney (`--load-type`, `--load-type-in SITE=TYPE`).
- **Sydney** had no g6-dedicated-4 or larger capacity on 22 November and refused g6-dedicated-2 part way on several attempts (AGENTS.md). Listener hosts are g6-dedicated-4 or -8 only (owner), so the plans keep g6-dedicated-4 there and treat the region as optional: `up --optional-sites sydney` creates Sydney's core first and goes on without it when it is refused. Each plan prints the lab without Sydney (its listeners, instances, vCPUs and cost), and the summary its $/h.
- **The LiveKit control**: one self-hosted SFU a region carrying 450 subscribers, its crowd on the listener hosts at 0.008 vCPU and 0.42 Mbit/s of public traffic each (`--lk-listeners-per-region`, `--lk-vcpu-per-listener`, `--lk-mbps`, `--no-livekit`). LiveKit Cloud's egress is billed by LiveKit, not Linode, and is not in these figures.
- **Spares**: 1 standby edge a region (created, held out: the autoscaler's first join and the edge killed mid-party is replaced from it), 2 edge slots and 1 forwarder slot (`dist-c`) a region that only the autoscaler creates (`--standby-edges`, `--edge-slots`, `--forwarder-slots`). Serving edges are sized for the plan's listeners, at least two a region.
- **Fixed nodes**: two forwarders a region (g6-dedicated-2), both contributors alone on a g6-dedicated-4 each, the source host (g6-dedicated-4), a view host a region (g6-dedicated-4) for the probes, LiveKit's Redis.
- **Traffic**: listener and view hosts read the edges over the region's VLAN (not billed). Public: 4 copies of the stream into each region outside London (both forwarders fed by both of London's; `--long-haul-copies`) and LiveKit's crowd. Each instance adds its plan's transfer to the account's monthly pool (4, 5 and 6 TB for g6-dedicated-2, -4 and -8, by the hour of a 730-hour month); past the pool $0.005 a GB.
- **Cost**: every instance billed whole hours, at least one (`--hours`, default 4: the watchdog's hours). Prices of 22 November: g6-dedicated-2 $0.054, g6-dedicated-4 $0.108, g6-dedicated-8 $0.216 an hour.
- **Not counted**: LL-HLS readers (scale-run sizes them), the view hosts' probes (a few listeners), more long-haul flows than the copies above (workstream D), LiveKit Cloud.

## Budget

About $300 for the whole work (owner, 23 November), $41.00 spent (`.resilient/ledger.md`: $40.65), $45.40 held for others (the cloud session's $40 and the stall hunt with one repeat, $5.40), and the proving run before the party: every node and slot of `topology-proving.json` for 10 billed hours (two labs of 5 hours: `deploy/low-latency/SCALE.md`, "The proving run"), $36.18. **$177.42 is left for the party** (`--budget-usd`, `--spent-usd`, `--reserved-usd`, `--proving-hours`). Every plan below is judged at its worst: every slot made once and alive for the whole run, and every GB of public traffic past the pool. A slot deleted and made again bills another whole hour; the worst does not count that, and the lab tool's budget guard bounds it.
